# 🔮 OmniAegis - Autonomous Cross-Chain Asset Shield

> **AI-Powered Active Defense for Your Multi-Chain Portfolio**

## 🚀 Introduction

**OmniAegis** is a revolutionary, AI-native active defense system built on **ZetaChain** that dynamically monitors and protects your assets across any blockchain. It transforms passive holdings into **intelligently defended positions** that can autonomously react to threats.

### The Problem
- **Multi-Chain Complexity**: Managing security across 5+ chains is impossible manually.
- **Reactive Protection**: Existing solutions act AFTER exploits occur.
- **Fragmented Tools**: No unified system for cross-chain risk monitoring and execution.

### Our Solution
An end-to-end system that **perceives, analyzes, and acts** in real-time:
1. **Perception**: AI models monitor real-time data across all connected chains
2. **Analysis**: Risk engines evaluate protocol health, market conditions, and threat intelligence
3. **Action**: Through ZetaChain's omnichain contracts, execute defensive maneuvers automatically

## ✨ Core Features

### 🛡️ **Active Threat Response**
- **Automatic Liquidation Protection**: Detect dangerous health factors and rebalance positions cross-chain before liquidation
- **Gas Crisis Evacuation**: Move high-value assets from congested chains during network stress
- **Protocol Abandonment**: Withdraw funds from potentially compromised protocols within seconds

### 🧠 **AI Risk Intelligence**
- Multi-dimensional risk scoring (protocol, chain, market, social)
- Predictive analytics for emerging threats
- Customizable risk tolerance profiles (Conservative/Balanced/Aggressive)

### ⛓️ **ZetaChain-Powered Execution**
- **Single-transaction cross-chain actions** (no multi-step manual processes)
- **Native Bitcoin protection** - yes, we secure your Bitcoin on its native chain
- **Atomic execution** - all steps succeed or everything reverts
